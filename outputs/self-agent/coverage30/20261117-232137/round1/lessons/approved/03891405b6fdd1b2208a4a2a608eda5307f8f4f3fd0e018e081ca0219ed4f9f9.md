# Verify promotion expiration before confirming date

## When
A customer asks to confirm a specific expiration date for a discount code or promotion, and retrieved results show the applied discount but omit its end date.

## Do
State only the verified discount details present in official results and clearly express uncertainty regarding the exact expiration date.

## Otherwise
State an unverified promotion expiration date or promise that an offer lasts until a specific day.
