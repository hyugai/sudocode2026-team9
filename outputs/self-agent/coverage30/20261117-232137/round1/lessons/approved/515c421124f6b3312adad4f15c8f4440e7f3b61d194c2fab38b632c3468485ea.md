# Do Not Invent Customer History Details

## When
Answering a customer referencing a past conversation or order details when tool results or sources do not contain previous record data

## Do
State clearly that the specific past details are not found in the records and ask the customer to provide them directly

## Otherwise
State unverified past selections or products as existing customer history
