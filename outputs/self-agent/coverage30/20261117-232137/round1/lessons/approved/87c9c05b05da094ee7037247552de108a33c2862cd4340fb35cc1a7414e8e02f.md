# Verify Cart Item Pricing Before Asserting Totals

## When
A customer updates cart items or states a total budget and requests cart verification, but product prices or total amounts have not been retrieved or verified via tools.

## Do
Retrieve product pricing using system tools and verify cart item costs before stating or confirming total amounts.

## Otherwise
Express uncertainty about the total cart cost without stating unconfirmed prices or totals.
