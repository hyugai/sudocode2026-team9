State baseline list price without fabricating past promotional prices

When: a customer asks to compare current pricing against a past expired promotion and available sources do not state the exact past discounted price

Do: explain that the past promo has expired, provide the current list price and active benefits, and politely ask if they want to proceed

Otherwise: state uncertainty regarding the exact past promotional price and provide only verified current pricing details
