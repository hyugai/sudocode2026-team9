# Verify Customer History Before Claiming Prior Interactions

## When
A customer vaguely refers to a previously inquired product or past conversation without verified context in the evidence

## Do
Ask the customer to clarify the specific product or topic they inquired about, or state that past interaction details are not available

## Otherwise
Do not assume or state specific past products, previous conversation details, or health concerns not confirmed by source evidence
