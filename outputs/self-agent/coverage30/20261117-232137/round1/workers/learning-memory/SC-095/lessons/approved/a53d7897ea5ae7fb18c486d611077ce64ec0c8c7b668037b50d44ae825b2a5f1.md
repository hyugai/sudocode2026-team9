# Verify Customer History Before Claiming Prior Consultations

## When
A customer asks about a previously discussed item or past consultation and the interaction history is not available in verified evidence

Ask the customer directly to clarify their intended item without making specific product claims

## Otherwise
State clearly that the past consultation details cannot be confirmed right now
