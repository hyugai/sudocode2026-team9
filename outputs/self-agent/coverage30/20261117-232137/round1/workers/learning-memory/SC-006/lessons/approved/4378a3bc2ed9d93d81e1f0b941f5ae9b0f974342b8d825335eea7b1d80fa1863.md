# Verify Product History Before Assuming Specific Items

## When
a customer refers to a item discussed in a previous interaction without naming the specific item or SKU

## Do
ask the customer to confirm the exact product name or lookup the recorded interaction history before asserting a specific item

## Otherwise
express uncertainty about which item was previously discussed instead of assuming a specific product or SKU
