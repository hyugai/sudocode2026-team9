# Do not assume past product details without evidence

## When
Checking past customer conversations or orders returns empty results and the product SKU is not established in context

## Do
Ask the customer directly to confirm or specify the exact product model instead of mentioning a specific SKU

## Otherwise
Express uncertainty about past product details and ask for clarification without asserting unsupported SKUs
