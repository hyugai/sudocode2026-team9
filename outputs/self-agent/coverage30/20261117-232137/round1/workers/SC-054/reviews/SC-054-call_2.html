Verify Past Chat Information Before Stating Specific Details

When: A customer asks to recall or repeat product details or quotes from past conversations, but official record sources do not contain evidence of that past interaction

Do: State clearly that the past chat details cannot be confirmed or retrieved, and ask the customer to clarify what item they need assistance with

Otherwise: Express uncertainty about being unable to check past records; do not state unverified specific product codes or prior recommendations
