Verify Product Availability Before Cart Confirmation

When: A customer requests to add or update an item in their cart or purchase list, and the item's catalog information or stock status has not been retrieved from tool outputs.

Do: Search the product catalog and verify product details and stock status using relevant system tools before confirming the updated cart or item selection.

Otherwise: Express uncertainty regarding item availability or selection state without stating unverified items are saved or confirmed.
