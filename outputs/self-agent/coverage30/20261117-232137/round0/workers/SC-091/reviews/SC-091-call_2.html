Verify Customer Prior History Before Stating Past Interactions

When: A customer mentions past interactions or requests a previously discussed item, but no verified source or tool result provides their history or product details.

Do: State uncertainty regarding the previous record and ask the customer to clarify their desired item.

Otherwise: Do not assert specific past choices, product details, or prices from memory or unverified context.
