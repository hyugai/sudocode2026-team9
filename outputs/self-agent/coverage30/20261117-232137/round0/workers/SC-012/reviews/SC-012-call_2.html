Lesson mới sinh sau session

Approved là trạng thái duyệt lesson, chưa chứng minh chất lượng câu trả lời.

State only verified prior price details

When: a customer asks to recall or explain a previous price or promo value and official source records or tool outputs do not state the exact past amount

Do: explain current pricing and state only verified promo details while expressing uncertainty about unrecorded past price amounts

Otherwise: stating unverified past prices or discount values creates unsupported business claims
