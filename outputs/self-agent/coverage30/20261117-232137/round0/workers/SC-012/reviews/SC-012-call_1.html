Verify promotion expiration dates before confirming

When: a customer asks to confirm a promotion's specific expiration date and retrieved records do not contain the exact end date

Do: state only the confirmed discount details and express uncertainty regarding the exact end date without confirming or inventing a specific date

Otherwise: confirming an unverified expiration date risks stating unsupported claims

Inject tại: Chưa thấy trong trace

Otherwise có thể đang mô tả hành vi cần cấm; cần đọc lại ngữ nghĩa.
