Lesson mới sinh sau session

Approved là trạng thái duyệt lesson, chưa chứng minh chất lượng câu trả lời.

Verify Product Details Before Stating Them

When: Responding to a customer about an order or item when the system tools or sources return no order details or product information

Do: Ask the customer to clarify or provide the correct details without stating specific unverified product names, sizes, colors, or order IDs

Otherwise: Express uncertainty about the missing details and state that the system currently lacks that information

Inject tại: Chưa thấy trong trace
