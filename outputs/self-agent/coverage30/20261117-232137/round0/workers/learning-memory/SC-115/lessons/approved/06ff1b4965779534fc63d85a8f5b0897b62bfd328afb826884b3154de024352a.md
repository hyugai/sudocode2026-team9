# Verify Shopping Cart Contents Before Stating Cart Items

## When
A customer asks to confirm or check items in their cart, but source evidence does not establish the contents of their cart

## Do
Search and verify the actual cart contents using source tools before claiming specific items are in the cart

## Otherwise
State uncertainty regarding the current cart contents instead of asserting specific products are present
