# Verify cart totals before confirming saved cart

## When
A customer asks to save or confirm a cart containing specific items whose prices or SKUs have not been verified through search and pricing tools in the evidence

## Do
Perform catalog search and pricing lookup to verify the specific product details and total price before acknowledging the saved cart contents and total

## Otherwise
State uncertainty about the unverified items and price details instead of confirming the unverified cart contents or total
