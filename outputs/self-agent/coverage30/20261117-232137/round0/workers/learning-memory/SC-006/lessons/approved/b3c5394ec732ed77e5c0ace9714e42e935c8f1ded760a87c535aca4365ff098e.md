# Verify Product Names from Verified Evidence

## When
a customer refers to a previously discussed item without naming it explicitly and there is no evidence establishing the exact product details

## Do
ask the customer to confirm the specific product name or details before assuming or stating any product identity

## Otherwise
state clearly that the product details are not available in the system context instead of guessing product names
